# Monthly Team Meeting - Parking and Morale Discussion

## Summary
The team reviewed attendance, introduced new members, and focused on resolving car parking conflicts. They agreed on a priority parking system, allocated two spaces to Sue Carpenter and Jason Somerville, set three visitor spaces, and decided to circulate a staff list outlining the new parking rules. They also discussed staff morale but no decision was reached. Action items include preparing and distributing the parking list and posting signage. The team discussed low staff morale linked to sales drops, sickness, new software training gaps, and recent restructuring. Various morale‑boosting activities were suggested and a staff vote was proposed. IT backup and power issues were reviewed, with agreement to pay electricity bills on time. A follow‑up meeting on training was planned, and action items were assigned to gather activity ideas and address the power issue. The team reviewed a confusing financial report, clarified that the key figure is 11, confirmed the company is in the black but down compared to last year, and noted general cleanliness problems including kitchen plates and the shower room, as well as a possible dishwasher issue. No decisions were finalized; the cleanliness issues were agreed to be scheduled for the next meeting.
