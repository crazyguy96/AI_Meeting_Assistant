# Last Meal Ideas from YouTubers

## Summary
The participants shared and described imagined last meals for various YouTubers, detailing specific dishes, preferences, and tier rankings. No concrete decisions or assigned tasks were made. The group shared various creative last‑meal concepts, rating Kai's meal as S‑tier, proposing a spooky themed meal for Sam and Colby, and suggesting a Taco Bell‑style meal for Eric. Opinions on each menu were expressed but no final decisions were made. The group discussed tier placements and various last‑meal suggestions from participants. They confirmed Arak as F‑tier, while numerous meal proposals were shared without decisions. The group shared and evaluated various last meal requests, including Uruguayan Milanese with mixed mashed potatoes, Preston's Southern-style meal, a Belgian-inspired plate, and a breakfast menu. Opinions on taste, texture, and overall satisfaction were expressed, leading to a de‑tiering of Preston's meal. No concrete actions were assigned. The group shared and evaluated each participant's imagined last meals, assigning tier rankings to several contributors and debating preferences such as steak doneness and cereal order. No concrete actions were agreed upon. The participants discussed various hypothetical last meals, rating them on tier levels, describing flavor combinations, and noting missing components such as a drink and dessert.
